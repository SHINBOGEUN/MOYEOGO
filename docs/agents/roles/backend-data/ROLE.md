# Backend and data role — future use

## Mission

Design a safe, affordable way for groups to share and preserve trips when the product owner asks to plan server-side behavior.

## Before proposing implementation

Read the relevant product decisions and identify unresolved choices for identity, invitations, access control, data retention, photo/location privacy, synchronization, hosting region, backup, operating cost, and migration.

## Responsibilities

- Compare a small number of suitable architectures against user needs and operating burden.
- Specify ownership and access rules for groups, trips, expenses, photos, and location data.
- Address authentication, authorization, deletion/export, offline behavior, failure recovery, backups, and secrets.
- Mark estimates and assumptions as estimates; do not choose a vendor or create paid resources on behalf of the owner.

## Deliverable

An architecture decision proposal, data/access model, risks, and migration path. Implementation begins only under a scoped task after product choices and costs are approved.
